WooCommerce cart empty after adding a product

The shop says "added to your cart", but the cart page shows Your cart is currently empty, the cart icon stays at 0, or the cart empties when the customer reaches checkout. Customers who see this leave. It is almost always one of two things: a cached copy of the cart page, or the shop losing track of who the customer is. Two checks tell you which.

Customer sees

What the checks found


  





Check 1: is the cart page cached?


Open the shop in a private window, add a product, then open the cart page with ?nocache=1 added to its address (for example /cart/?nocache=1). If the product shows there but not on the plain /cart/ address, a cache is serving an old, empty copy of the page. Look at every cache layer you have: a caching plugin (WP Rocket, LiteSpeed Cache, W3 Total Cache, WP Super Cache), your host's server cache, and Cloudflare. Each must leave the cart, checkout and my account pages uncached, and must not cache pages for visitors who carry the woocommerce_items_in_cart or wp_woocommerce_session_ cookies.



Check 2: does the session cookie survive?


Still in the private window, press F12, open Application (Chrome) or Storage (Firefox) and then Cookies. After adding a product you should see a cookie starting with wp_woocommerce_session_. If it never appears, or appears and then disappears on the next page, the shop cannot remember the cart. Common reasons: the site mixes www and non-www or http and https addresses, so the cookie is set on one and read on the other; a cookie consent plugin blocks WooCommerce cookies until the visitor clicks accept; or Cloudflare or a security plugin strips cookies.

Check it worked


Purge every cache, then in a fresh private window add two different products, open the cart, change a quantity, and go through to checkout. The cart count, the cart page and the checkout order summary should all agree. Repeat once on a phone over mobile data, since some caches only serve phones.



What not to do


    
  
  • Do not turn caching off for the whole site to fix it. Exclude the cart pages and logged-in or cart-holding visitors; the rest of the shop should stay fast.
    • 
  
  • Do not empty the woocommerce_sessions table by hand on a busy shop. Every live cart in it disappears.
    • 
  
  • Do not paste your hosting or WordPress password into a forum to get help with this.

Android phones can't tap a plain-text number

Most people find a local business on a phone. If your number is printed as plain text, an iPhone guesses and links it for you, but on an Android phone tapping it does nothing. That visitor has to select it, copy it, open the dialer and paste. Many skip all that and call the next business on the list. This page turns the iPhone guess off, so on any phone the Before panel shows what an Android visitor gets. Try both below.

Before

Number is plain text
Rivera Plumbing

(847) 555-0142
Tap the number above.

After

Number is a tap-to-call link
Rivera Plumbing
Same-day repairs across the north suburbs.

Call us:
(847) 555-0142
Tap the number above.

  • WordPress / Elementor: select the number, add a link, type tel:+18475550142. Do the header, footer and contact page.
  • Wix / Squarespace: select the number, choose Link, then Phone, and enter the number.
  • Check it by tapping the number on your own phone. The dialer should open with the number filled in.

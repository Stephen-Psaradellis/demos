Booking & arrival

How do I book a visit?

Book online in about two minutes, or call our office. For anything urgent such as a burst pipe or no heat, please call rather than booking online.

How fast can you get here in an emergency?

Our average emergency response across Riverton is under 60 minutes, 24 hours a day, including weekends and holidays.

Will I know when the technician is coming?

Yes. You choose a two-hour arrival window, and we text you with your technician's name and photo when they are about 20 minutes away.

Prices & payment

Do you charge for quotes?

Quotes for planned work such as a new water heater are free. For repairs, the call-out fee covers diagnosis and is waived if you go ahead with the repair.

Do you charge by the hour?

No. We use flat-rate pricing: you approve a fixed price in writing before work starts, and that price does not change even if the job takes longer.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major credit and debit cards, bank transfer and cash. Financing is available on installations over $1,000, subject to approval.

The work itself

Are your technicians licensed and insured?

Every technician is state-licensed, background-checked and covered by our liability insurance. Our licence number appears on every quote and invoice.

Do you pull permits?

Yes. Where a permit is required, such as water heater or gas line work, we file it and arrange the inspection. The cost is included in your quote.

Will you leave a mess?

Never. We wear shoe covers, lay drop sheets and clean up fully before we leave. Old parts and appliances are hauled away and recycled.

Guarantees

What does your guarantee cover?

All parts we supply and all labour we perform are guaranteed for two years. If something we fixed fails, we come back and put it right at no charge.

What if you are late?

If we arrive after your booked window without letting you know in advance, the call-out fee is free.

Still have a question?

Call (555) 010-0142 or send us a message and we will reply within one business hour.

FAQ

Questions we hear every day

Do you charge for quotes?

Quotes for planned work such as a new water heater are always free. For repairs, the call-out fee covers the diagnosis and is waived if you go ahead with the repair.

How fast can you get here in an emergency?

Our average emergency response across Riverton is under 60 minutes, day or night. Call us rather than booking online for anything urgent.

Are your plumbers licensed and insured?

Yes. Every technician is state-licensed, background-checked and covered by our liability insurance. Our licence number is printed on every invoice.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major cards, bank transfer and cash. Financing is available on installations over $1,000.

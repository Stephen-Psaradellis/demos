Our story

From one van to the valley's most-reviewed plumber

Tom Keystone spent twelve years working for national franchises before going out on his own. He was tired of being told to sell customers things they did not need, so he built a company where technicians are paid for good work, not for upsells.

Today we are a team of 18 licensed plumbers and heating engineers, still run from the same workshop on Alder Street. Most of our work comes from repeat customers and their neighbours, which is exactly how we like it.

What we stand for

Four promises on every job

Honest, fixed prices

We price the job, not the hour. The number we write down is the number you pay, even if the job takes longer than planned.

Respect for your time

Two-hour arrival windows, a text when we are on the way, and a free call-out if we are ever late.

Work that lasts

Quality parts, fitted to code and inspected. That is why we can guarantee every job for two full years.

Part of the community

We sponsor two youth teams and fix heating for local seniors free every winter through our Warm Homes programme.

Indonesian → English: translation sample

A short business notice (written for this sample, no client material). Meaning first, natural English, names and figures kept exact, with notes where a choice was made.

Indonesian (source)

PEMBERITAHUAN PERUBAHAN JADWAL PENGIRIMAN

Dengan hormat,

Sehubungan dengan libur nasional pada tanggal 17 Agustus, kami informasikan bahwa pengiriman barang untuk pesanan yang masuk setelah tanggal 14 Agustus pukul 15.00 WIB akan diproses mulai tanggal 18 Agustus.

Pesanan dengan pembayaran di muka tetap kami prioritaskan. Apabila Bapak/Ibu memerlukan pengiriman lebih cepat, silakan menghubungi tim layanan pelanggan kami melalui surel atau WhatsApp sebelum batas waktu tersebut.

Atas perhatian dan kerja samanya, kami ucapkan terima kasih.

Hormat kami,
Bagian Logistik

English (translation)

NOTICE OF CHANGE TO DELIVERY SCHEDULE

Dear Customer,

Due to the national holiday on 17 August, orders received after 3:00 p.m. Western Indonesia Time (WIB) on 14 August will be processed for delivery starting 18 August.

Prepaid orders will continue to receive priority. If you need faster delivery, please contact our customer service team by email or WhatsApp before that cutoff.

Thank you for your attention and cooperation.

Sincerely,
Logistics Department

Translator's notes

  • Dengan hormat / Bapak/Ibu - formal openings with no direct English equivalent; rendered as "Dear Customer" and "you" rather than a literal "Sir/Madam".
  • Pukul 15.00 WIB - kept the time zone and spelled it out once (Western Indonesia Time), so a reader abroad cannot misread the cutoff.
  • Atas perhatian dan kerja samanya - a set closing phrase; translated by function ("Thank you for your attention and cooperation"), not word by word.

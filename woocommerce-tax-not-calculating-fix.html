WooCommerce tax not calculating at checkout

The cart and checkout show Tax: $0.00, or no tax line at all, even though you set up tax rates. Or tax shows for some customers and not others, or only after the address is typed in full. Every order placed like this is one you owe the tax on yourself. WooCommerce adds tax only when taxes are switched on, the product is taxable, and a rate row matches the address it is told to use. Below is a rebuilt example (not a real client), the two checks that find which of those failed, and the fix for each one.

Checkout totals

What the checks found


  





Check 1: is tax on, and which address does it use


Go to WooCommerce > Settings > General and make sure Enable tax rates and calculations is ticked; without it the Tax tab does not even appear. Then open WooCommerce > Settings > Tax and note two settings: Calculate tax based on (customer shipping address, billing address, or shop base address) and Display prices during cart and checkout. If an automated tax service (WooCommerce Tax, TaxJar, Avalara) is switched on, it replaces your rate tables, so its own connection status matters more than the rows.



Check 2: does a rate row match this product and this address


Open one product that showed no tax and look at Product data > General: Tax status should be Taxable and note its Tax class (Standard, Reduced rate, Zero rate or your own). Then open that class's table under the Tax tab and compare its rows with a real customer address: country code, state code, ZIP or postcode, city. Check one with a real order: in a private window, add that product and enter the address in full at checkout. Note whether tax appears before or after you type the ZIP.

Check it worked


In a private window, add a taxable product, go to checkout and enter a full address in each state or country you collect tax for. The tax line should match the rate you set. Then place one real order and open it in WooCommerce > Orders: the tax line must be on the order itself, not only on the checkout screen. Orders already placed without tax are not fixed by this; they stay as they were.



What not to do


    
  
  • Do not add the tax into the product price by hand to make it show. You then charge tax on tax once rates start working, and your reports lose the tax figure.
    • 
  
  • Do not put a single * rate for every country to make a number appear. You will charge tax to customers you should not, which is harder to undo than missing it.
    • 
  
  • Do not run two tax services at once (a plugin plus your own table). One overrides the other and you cannot tell which number is real.
    •

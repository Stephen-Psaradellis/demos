WooCommerce sale price not showing

You typed a sale price, maybe scheduled it for the weekend, and the shop still shows the full price: no crossed-out price, no Sale! badge, or the sale shows on the product page but the cart charges the old price. Customers who came for the sale leave. Two checks tell you whether WooCommerce thinks the sale is on, and if it does, what is showing the old price.

Product page

What the checks found


  





Check 1: is the sale really on for this product


Open the product and go to Product data > General. The Sale price must be lower than the Regular price; a sale price equal to or above it is ignored. If you clicked Schedule, read the two dates: the sale starts at 00:00 on the From date and ends at the end of the To date, in the time zone set under Settings > General > Timezone, so a sale dated today can still be hours away. For a variable product the General tab has no price at all: open Variations and expand each one, because every variation carries its own sale price and dates.



Check 2: is something showing an old price


Open the product in a private window (logged out) and add it to the cart. If the cart has the sale price but the shop page does not, a page cache or CDN is serving a copy saved before the sale. If both show the full price but the admin shows the sale, go to WooCommerce > Status > Tools and run Clear transients and Regenerate the product lookup tables. If the price changes depending on who is logged in or what is in the cart, a pricing, role-based or discount plugin is overriding it; deactivate it on a staging copy and look again.

Check it worked


In a private window, the shop page and product page should show the regular price crossed out next to the sale price, with the Sale! badge if your theme uses one. Add it to the cart: the line total should use the sale price. For a scheduled sale, check again just after the start time and just after the end time.



What not to do


    
  
  • Do not lower the Regular price to fake a sale. When the sale ends you have to remember every product you changed, and customers never see a saving.
    • 
  
  • Do not turn caching off for good. Purge it when prices change.
    • 
  
  • Do not stack a discount plugin on top of WooCommerce sale prices without testing the cart. Two discounts on one item can sell below cost.

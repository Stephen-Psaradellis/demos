Establishing a tungsten supply base in China

Sample of the research brief structure, with the regulatory facts that frame it. Sourced facts are cited; supplier rows in the full report are filled from company filings, websites and trade directories, each with its source.

1. Why the export rules come first

On 4 February 2025 China's Ministry of Commerce and General Administration of Customs placed tungsten-related items under export control (Decision No. 10 of 2025). Ammonium paratungstate (customs code 2841801000) is among the listed items, and exporters must obtain a dual-use export licence from the Ministry of Commerce before shipping. [1]

China remains the world's leading producer, importer and consumer of tungsten concentrates; USGS links the February 2025 controls, and higher US tariffs on several Chinese tungsten products from the end of 2024, to sharp price rises through 2025. [2]

Practical consequence: a manufacturer's production volume matters less than whether it already holds export licences for the specific product form you need (APT, tungsten oxide, powder, carbide, alloy) - and how long its licence applications have taken.

Starting points for the long list include China's listed tungsten groups (for example Xiamen Tungsten, China Tungsten High-Tech Materials, Jiangxi Tungsten Industry Group, Chongyi Zhangyuan Tungsten); each is verified against the criteria above before it reaches the shortlist.

4. What the full report delivers

  1. A scored shortlist of manufacturers with capacity, product forms, export-licence status, responsible-sourcing status and named contacts - every figure with its source.
  2. A plain-English summary of the export-licence process for the product form you need, and what it means for lead time.
  3. Recommendations: which two or three suppliers to approach first, a second-source option, and the questions to ask each before ordering samples.

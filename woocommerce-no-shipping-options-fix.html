WooCommerce "No shipping options were found"

A customer adds something to the cart, types their address, and the cart or checkout says "No shipping options were found for [address]" or "There are no shipping options available. Please ensure that your address has been entered correctly". They cannot place the order, and you only find out when someone emails you or sales stop. Almost always the shop has no shipping method that matches that address and those products. Below is a rebuilt example (not a real client), the two checks that find the cause, and the fix for each one.

Checkout

What the checks found


  





Check 1: which zone does the address land in?


Open WooCommerce > Settings > Shipping > Shipping zones. WooCommerce reads the list from the top and uses the first zone whose regions match the customer's address (country, state, or ZIP / postcode). Only the methods in that one zone are offered. If no zone matches, the "Rest of the world" zone at the bottom is used (it may be called "Locations not covered by your other zones"). Take the address that failed and work out which zone it hits first, and whether that zone has at least one enabled method.



Check 2: turn on shipping debug mode


Go to WooCommerce > Settings > Shipping > Shipping options (sometimes Shipping settings), tick Enable debug mode and save. Then, logged in as an admin, add the same product to the cart and enter the failing address. The cart now shows which zone matched and which methods were checked. This also turns off WooCommerce's shipping cache while it is on, so a cached answer cannot fool you. Turn it off again when you are done.

ZIP ranges use 90210...90299 (three dots) and wildcards like 902*, one per line. Then test that exact address again

Check it worked


Log out (or use a private window), add each kind of product to the cart, and enter one real address from every zone you serve, including one under any free-shipping minimum. Each should show at least one shipping price. Then turn debug mode off again.



What not to do


    
  
  • Do not put a catch-all "Free shipping" in "Rest of the world" to make the message go away. It hides the problem and you may end up shipping abroad for free.
    • 
  
  • Do not delete and rebuild every zone at once. Change one thing, test the failing address, and keep the rest.
    • 
  
  • Do not leave debug mode on. Customers would see the debug notes, and the shipping cache stays off.
    •

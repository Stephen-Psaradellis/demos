WooCommerce products not showing on the shop page

The shop page says "No products were found matching your selection", or a category page is empty, or a product you just added shows in the dashboard but not to customers. Nobody can buy what they cannot see. The cause is nearly always one of a few things: the product's own status or visibility, stock settings, the page WooCommerce thinks is the shop, a lookup table that was never rebuilt after an import, or a cached copy of the page. Below is a rebuilt example (not a real client), the two checks that find the cause, and the fix for each one.

Shop page

What the checks found


  





Check 1: open one missing product in the dashboard


Go to Products > All Products and find a product customers cannot see. Look at four things in its row and edit screen: the status (Published, not Draft, Pending or Private), the Catalog visibility in the Publish box (it should say "Shop and search results"), the stock status, and whether it has a price. Then open its own page with the View link while logged out, in a private window. If the product page opens but the shop page leaves it out, the problem is visibility or the shop's lists; if the product page itself is missing, it is the status.



Check 2: compare logged in, logged out, and the settings pages


In a private window, logged out, open the shop page and one category. If you see products as an admin but customers see none, the page they get is a cached copy or a plugin hides products from visitors. Then open WooCommerce > Settings > Products and check which page is set as the Shop page, and WooCommerce > Settings > Products > Inventory for "Hide out of stock items from the catalog".



What each answer means





  
What you seeCauseFix

  
The product is Draft, Pending, Private or ScheduledOnly published products appear in the shop; a CSV import or a bulk edit often leaves them as draftsTick the products in All Products, choose Edit from Bulk actions, set Status to Published and update. Check the publish date is not in the future

  
Catalog visibility says "Hidden" or "Search results only"The product is set to stay out of the shop lists on purposeEdit the product, click Edit next to Catalog visibility in the Publish box, choose "Shop and search results" and update

  
Only out-of-stock products are missing"Hide out of stock items from the catalog" is ticked, or stock was imported as 0Untick that option in Settings > Products > Inventory, or set the right stock numbers (or allow backorders) on the products

  
The whole shop page is empty, or shows a plain page with no productsThe page set as the Shop page is wrong, deleted or in the trash, or a page builder replaced the shop with a static layoutIn Settings > Products, pick the right Shop page (it can be empty: WooCommerce fills it). If a builder template is used for it, make sure it holds the products widget or loop

  
Products show, but category pages or filters return "No products were found"The product lookup tables or term counts are out of date, usually after an import, a migration or a database restoreIn WooCommerce > Status > Tools, run "Regenerate the product lookup tables", "Recount terms" and "Clear transients". Check each product is in the category (not only "Uncategorized")

  
Admins see products, logged-out visitors see none or an old listA cached copy of the shop page from before the products were published (caching plugin, the host's cache or Cloudflare)Purge every cache layer and reload logged out. If it comes back, set the cache to expire shop and category pages when a product is saved

  
Products vanished after installing or updating a plugin (members, B2B, wholesale, a filter plugin)That plugin hides products from guests or certain roles, or its filter query breaks the shop listCheck the plugin's visibility rules first. Use the Health Check plugin's troubleshooting mode to switch plugins off for yourself only and find the one that hides them

  
Products show in the dashboard but each product page gives a 404Permalinks were not refreshed after a change to the product base or a migrationOpen Settings > Permalinks and click Save without changing anything. See 404 errors if it stays







Check it worked


In a private window, logged out, open the shop page, two categories and a search for one product name. Every product you meant to sell should appear with a price and an Add to cart or Select options button. Then do the same on a phone, since mobile visitors can get a different cached copy.



What not to do


    
  
  • Do not re-import the whole catalog to "refresh" it. You get duplicates; fix the status, visibility or lookup tables instead.
    • 
  
  • Do not delete and recreate the Shop page while the shop is live without setting it again in WooCommerce's settings straight after.
    • 
  
  • Do not switch plugins off one by one on the live shop while customers browse it. Use troubleshooting mode or a staging copy.
    •

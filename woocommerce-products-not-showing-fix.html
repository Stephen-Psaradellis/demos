WooCommerce products not showing on the shop page

The shop page says "No products were found matching your selection", or a category page is empty, or a product you just added shows in the dashboard but not to customers. Nobody can buy what they cannot see. The cause is nearly always one of a few things: the product's own status or visibility, stock settings, the page WooCommerce thinks is the shop, a lookup table that was never rebuilt after an import, or a cached copy of the page. Below is a rebuilt example (not a real client), the two checks that find the cause, and the fix for each one.

Shop page

What the checks found


  





Check 1: open one missing product in the dashboard


Go to Products > All Products and find a product customers cannot see. Look at four things in its row and edit screen: the status (Published, not Draft, Pending or Private), the Catalog visibility in the Publish box (it should say "Shop and search results"), the stock status, and whether it has a price. Then open its own page with the View link while logged out, in a private window. If the product page opens but the shop page leaves it out, the problem is visibility or the shop's lists; if the product page itself is missing, it is the status.



Check 2: compare logged in, logged out, and the settings pages


In a private window, logged out, open the shop page and one category. If you see products as an admin but customers see none, the page they get is a cached copy or a plugin hides products from visitors. Then open WooCommerce > Settings > Products and check which page is set as the Shop page, and WooCommerce > Settings > Products > Inventory for "Hide out of stock items from the catalog".

If a builder template is used for it, make sure it holds the products widget or loop

Check it worked


In a private window, logged out, open the shop page, two categories and a search for one product name. Every product you meant to sell should appear with a price and an Add to cart or Select options button. Then do the same on a phone, since mobile visitors can get a different cached copy.



What not to do


    
  
  • Do not re-import the whole catalog to "refresh" it. You get duplicates; fix the status, visibility or lookup tables instead.
    • 
  
  • Do not delete and recreate the Shop page while the shop is live without setting it again in WooCommerce's settings straight after.
    • 
  
  • Do not switch plugins off one by one on the live shop while customers browse it. Use troubleshooting mode or a staging copy.
    •

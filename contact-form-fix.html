What was wrong (and still is)(and what changed)

  1. Sent "from" the visitor's address. The form mails From: dana.lee@gmail.com through your web host. Gmail's published policy says only Google may send as gmail.com, so your inbox provider rejects or spams it. Fix: send From your own domain (forms@yourbusiness.com, authorised in SPF/DKIM) with Reply-To set to the visitor, so Reply still reaches them.
  2. A phone rule that rejects normal numbers. "(555) 201-7788" fails a digits-only pattern, and the error sits below the fold on a phone. Fix: accept any format and strip it to digits on the server.
  3. No confirmation. The page just reloads, so visitors cannot tell whether it worked, and either give up or send three times. Fix: a clear thank-you that says when you will reply.
  4. No copy kept. When mail fails, the message is gone. Fix: every submission is also saved on the site (WordPress: the form plugin's entries list), so nothing is lost even if email has a bad day.
Demo built for a fixed-price fix: forms on WordPress (Contact Form 7, WPForms, Elementor), Wix and Squarespace fail in these same four ways. Nothing here sends real mail.

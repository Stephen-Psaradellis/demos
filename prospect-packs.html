How the list is made

  • Businesses come from public map listings; each site is fetched once over http and https.
  • Defects are checked, not guessed: certificate validity, mixed http content on the https page, the WordPress version published in the page source against WordPress.org's own version list, SPF and DMARC records over public DNS, phone layout, dead links.
  • No email addresses are sold, and any business that asked not to be contacted is removed.
  • Every row carries its scan date; rescans on request before you buy.

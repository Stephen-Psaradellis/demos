Action Scheduler: past-due actions found

Tools > Site Health shows "Action Scheduler: 1,204 past-due actions found; something may be wrong." Action Scheduler is the job queue WooCommerce and many plugins use for order emails, subscription renewals, stock updates, webhooks and Analytics imports. Past-due means those jobs are waiting and nothing is running them. It is almost always one of two things: the background runner never starts, or the jobs start and fail. Two checks tell you which.

Scheduled Actions screen

What the checks found


  





Check 1: does the runner ever start?


Open Tools > Scheduled Actions (on WooCommerce sites also under WooCommerce > Status > Scheduled Actions) and click Past-due. Note the oldest scheduled date. Wait five minutes, browse a couple of pages on the front of the site, then reload. If the count has not dropped and nothing moved to In-progress or Complete, the runner is not starting. The runner rides on WP-Cron, which only fires when someone visits the site and the site can call itself. Look in wp-config.php for define('DISABLE_WP_CRON', true);: if it is there, a real server cron job must replace it, and often it was never set up or stopped after a host move. Also check Site Health > Status for "could not complete a loopback request": a security plugin, password protection on the whole site, or a firewall rule blocking the site's own address stops every background job.



Check 2: do the jobs start and fail?


Still on Scheduled Actions, click Failed, and open the Log column of a few rows. Each failed job names its hook (for example woocommerce_run_product_attribute_lookup_update_callback or a subscription renewal) and the error it hit. A PHP fatal error, a timeout, or "action failed via async request" repeated on the same hook means one plugin's job is broken and keeps the queue busy. Hooks that belong to a plugin you have since deleted show "no callbacks are registered"; those can never run.

Check it worked


After the fix, open Past-due again and reload every few minutes. The count should fall steadily and new rows should appear under Complete with recent dates. Place a test order or trigger the plugin's own job, and confirm its action completes within a few minutes. Then check Site Health again: the past-due warning clears once the backlog is worked through.



What not to do


    
  
  • Do not empty the actionscheduler_actions table by hand. Pending renewals, order emails and webhooks in it are lost, and some shops then miss subscription payments.
    • 
  
  • Do not cancel all past-due actions to silence the warning. Fix why they are not running; cancel only rows whose plugin is gone.
    • 
  
  • Do not paste your hosting or WordPress password into a forum to get help with this.

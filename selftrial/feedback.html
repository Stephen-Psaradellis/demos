SelfTrial is a prototype diet and symptom tracker for people living with autoimmune disease. It lets you run a short, randomised diet experiment on yourself. Try it here (demo data loads on first open; your own entries stay on your device).

Who is asking: Stephen Psaradellis, entering SelfTrial in the NIH NOURISH Challenge. The app, this page and the questions were built with Claude, an AI system made by Anthropic. Answers are anonymous: we store only what you type below and the date, never your name, email, IP address or anything from the app. Please do not include names, contact details or your own medical details.

# Website problems fixed, on your existing site

Live site: https://stephen-psaradellis.github.io/demos/

Worked examples of common small-business website and email problems, each shown
broken and then fixed, plus free checks and the data behind them.

## Free tools and data

- [Email forgery check](https://stephen-psaradellis.github.io/demos/email-forgery-check.html) -
  type a domain and see whether mail in its name can be forged (SPF and DMARC,
  read in your browser over DNS-over-HTTPS; nothing is sent to us).
- [Small-business email forgery, 2026](https://stephen-psaradellis.github.io/demos/small-business-email-forgery-2026.html) -
  19,113 small-business mail domains checked: 57.2% publish no DMARC record.
- [GoDaddy email and SPF](https://stephen-psaradellis.github.io/demos/godaddy-email-spf.html) -
  83.2% of 1,017 GoDaddy-email domains publish no SPF, and the exact records that fix it.

## For web designers and agencies

- [Web design leads by city](https://stephen-psaradellis.github.io/demos/leads/) -
  25 US metros, local businesses whose site is down, insecure or broken, each row with
  the evidence. Ten rows free for any city and business type.
- [Prospect packs](https://stephen-psaradellis.github.io/demos/prospect-packs.html) -
  what a pack contains and how to order one.
- [Keystone HTML template](https://stephen-psaradellis.github.io/demos/keystone/) -
  16-page local-service business template; [buy on Gumroad](https://localwebkit.gumroad.com/l/keystone) ($29 one site, $69 agency).

Business data is derived from OpenStreetMap (ODbL); every pack carries the attribution.

WooCommerce product says out of stock (but it isn't)

You have the item on the shelf, yet the product page says Out of stock, the variation picker greys out a size with This product is currently out of stock and unavailable, the Add to cart button is gone, or the cart says Sorry, we do not have enough in stock to fulfil your order. Every one of those visitors wanted to buy. Two checks tell you whether WooCommerce really thinks the stock is gone or something else is showing an old answer.

Product page

What the checks found


  





Check 1: what does the product's own Inventory tab say


Open the product and go to Product data > Inventory. If Manage stock? (called Track stock quantity in newer versions) is ticked, the Quantity number decides everything: 0 or less is out of stock, whatever the Stock status says. If it is unticked, the Stock status dropdown decides. For a variable product, open Variations and expand each one: a variation can manage its own stock, and one at 0 is greyed out while the others sell. Also read WooCommerce > Settings > Products > Inventory: Out of stock threshold (a threshold of 2 makes 2 in stock show as out) and Hide out of stock items from the catalog.



Check 2: where did the stock go


Go to WooCommerce > Orders and filter by Pending payment and On hold. WooCommerce takes stock off when an order is placed, not when it is paid, and only puts it back when the order is cancelled. Hold stock (minutes) in the Inventory settings is how long it waits before cancelling an unpaid order; if it is empty, abandoned checkouts keep the stock forever. Then add the product to a cart in a private window. If WooCommerce now sells it but the page still said out of stock, a cache served an old copy of the page.

One size or colour greyed out, "currently out of stock and unavailable"That variation manages its own stock at 0, or has no price (a variation without a price cannot be bought)Open Variations, set the variation's quantity or untick its own Manage stock, and give it a price

  
Stock keeps vanishing with no paid ordersPending payment or Failed orders from abandoned or declined checkouts are holding it, and Hold stock is emptySet Hold stock to 60 minutes, then cancel the old Pending orders so the stock goes back

Check it worked


Open the product in a private window (logged out). The page should say In stock or show the count, every variation you have should be selectable, and Add to cart should put it in the cart without the "not enough in stock" message. Then go back to Inventory and check the Quantity did not drop just from adding it to the cart; it only drops when an order is placed.



What not to do


    
  
  • Do not untick Manage stock on every product to make the message go away. You will sell items you do not have and spend the week refunding.
    • 
  
  • Do not delete old Pending payment orders. Cancel them, so WooCommerce puts the stock back and the record stays.
    • 
  
  • Do not set a huge fake quantity like 999 as the standing fix. The next real stock count is wrong from day one and your low-stock emails stop meaning anything.

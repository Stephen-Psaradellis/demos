WooCommerce coupon not working

A customer types the code from your email or flyer and the cart says Coupon "SAVE10" is not valid, Coupon "SAVE10" does not exist!, Sorry, this coupon is not applicable to selected products, or it says applied but the discount is $0.00. Or there is no coupon box at checkout at all. Every one of those customers was ready to pay and many leave. Two checks tell you whether WooCommerce is refusing the coupon on purpose or something else is getting in the way.

Cart

What the checks found


  





Check 1: are coupons switched on, and is this coupon published


Go to WooCommerce > Settings > General and find Enable the use of coupon codes. If it is unticked there is no coupon box on the cart or checkout and every code fails. Then open Marketing > Coupons and find the code. It must be Published, not Draft, Pending or in the Trash, and the code the customer types must match it letter for letter (spaces count; WooCommerce ignores upper and lower case). A coupon you cannot find there is the "does not exist" message.



Check 2: do the coupon's own rules refuse this cart


Open the coupon and read three tabs. General: the discount type and amount, and the Coupon expiry date. Usage restriction: minimum and maximum spend, Individual use only, Exclude sale items, the products and categories it applies to or excludes, and Allowed emails. Usage limits: limit per coupon and per user, and how many times it has already been used. Now compare with the cart that failed: one expired date, one sale item, one product outside the listed categories or one customer email that is not on the allowed list is enough. WooCommerce's message usually names which rule it was, so write the exact message down before you change anything.



What each answer means

No coupon box on the cart or checkoutCoupons are switched off, or the theme or a checkout plugin hides the fieldTick Enable the use of coupon codes; if it is ticked, switch to a default theme for one test to see whether the theme removed it

  
"Coupon does not exist"A typo, a space at the end of the code, the coupon is in Draft or Trash, or it was made on a staging copy of the site and never on the live shopPublish the coupon on the live site and send customers the exact code; avoid codes with O and 0 or I and 1 that are easy to mistype

  
"This coupon has expired"The expiry date has passed; the date runs on the site's time zone, so a coupon can expire hours early or lateMove the date, and check Settings > General time zone is your own city, not UTC

  
"The minimum spend for this coupon is..."Minimum spend counts the cart before tax and shippingLower the minimum or say "over $50 before shipping" in the promotion

  
"Not applicable to selected products" or a $0.00 discountExclude sale items is ticked and the cart is all sale items, the products or categories list leaves these out, or a variation's parent product is listed instead of the variation (or the reverse)Untick Exclude sale items if you meant to allow it, add the missing categories, and test with the exact product the customer had

  
"Usage limit has been reached" or "not valid for you"Limit per coupon or per user is used up (failed and cancelled orders can count too), or Allowed emails does not include the customer's billing emailRaise or clear the limit, and clear Allowed emails unless the coupon really is for named people

  
"Sorry, coupon has already been applied and cannot be used in conjunction with other coupons"Individual use only is ticked on one of the two couponsRemove the other coupon first, or untick Individual use only if stacking is fine

Check it worked


Log out (or use a private window), add the same product the customer had, and apply the code at the cart. The line under the subtotal should read the coupon name and the discount, and the order total should drop by that amount. Then go to checkout and check the discount is still there on the order review before you pay. Repeat once with a product on sale if the promotion should include sale items.



What not to do


    
  
  • Do not delete and remake the coupon to "reset" it. The usage count and the orders that used it are tied to the old one, and customers with the old code get "does not exist".
    • 
  
  • Do not hand out a manual refund for the discount as the standing workaround. It costs you a payment fee each time and the customer still saw the error at checkout.
    • 
  
  • Do not turn off the page cache for the whole site to fix one cart. Exclude the cart, checkout and account pages and keep the rest fast.

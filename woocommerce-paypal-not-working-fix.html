WooCommerce PayPal not working: no button, an error, or orders stuck

The PayPal button is missing from checkout, or customers click it and get Something went wrong, or they pay and the order sits on Pending payment while the money is in your PayPal account. Each of these has a short list of causes. Two checks tell you whether PayPal ever saw the payment, and whether it was able to tell your shop afterwards.

Customer sees

What the checks found


  





Check 1: did PayPal see the payment?


Log in to your PayPal business account and open Activity. Look for the customer's name or amount at the time they tried. If the payment is there and completed, the money arrived and only the shop was not told: go to Check 2. If nothing is there, the problem is on the checkout page itself. Open checkout in a private window, press F12, and look at the Console tab while the page loads and when you click the PayPal button. A red error mentioning paypal.com/sdk means the PayPal script was blocked or delayed. Also check you are not in sandbox mode with live customers, or live mode with sandbox test logins.



Check 2: can PayPal reach your shop?


With the official WooCommerce PayPal Payments plugin, go to WooCommerce > Settings > Payments > PayPal and open the connection or webhooks section. It shows whether webhooks are subscribed; if not, use the button there to resubscribe. Webhooks are the messages PayPal sends back to say a payment completed. They are refused when the site is behind a maintenance mode, a password, a firewall that blocks PayPal, or a security plugin that blocks wp-json. Then open WooCommerce > Status > Logs and read the newest PayPal log for an error line at the time of the failed order.

Check it worked


In a private window, put a cheap product in the cart and pay with PayPal. If you want to test without real money, switch the plugin to sandbox mode and use a PayPal sandbox buyer login, then switch back to live before you open the shop again. The order should move to Processing on its own within a minute, and the PayPal log should show the webhook arriving. Clear the site and host cache afterwards.



What not to do


    
  
  • Do not refund and re-charge customers whose orders are stuck on Pending payment. Check PayPal Activity first; the money is often already there.
    • 
  
  • Do not leave the plugin in sandbox mode on the live shop. Customers cannot pay with real PayPal accounts in sandbox.
    • 
  
  • Do not paste your PayPal password or API keys into a support forum or a chat with someone you do not know.

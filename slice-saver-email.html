How the 3,000-contact send would be set up

  1. Authentication first. Brevo domain authenticated (its DKIM record + Brevo code), SPF including Brevo's include, and a DMARC record starting at p=none with reports, moved to quarantine once reports are clean. Send from the brand domain, never a free mailbox.
  2. List hygiene. De-duplicate, remove role and malformed addresses, and verify the list before the first send; anything that hard-bounces is blocklisted automatically.
  3. Segments. Customers / warm leads / cold prospects as separate lists, plus attributes for store type and region so the copy and the offer can differ.
  4. Ramp, warmest first. Customers, then warm leads, then prospects in batches (for example 300 / 600 / 1,000 / rest over about two weeks), pausing if bounces pass 2% or complaints pass 0.1%.
  5. Reporting. Opens are unreliable since Apple Mail privacy, so the report leads with clicks, replies, sample requests and orders, by segment.

WooCommerce variations not showing

You set up a variable product with sizes or colours, and the product page shows no dropdowns, shows them with options greyed out, or the customer picks a size and Add to cart still says Please select some product options before adding this product to your cart. Nobody can buy it. Two checks tell you whether the variations themselves are incomplete, or whether the page is failing to run the script that handles them.

Product page

What the checks found


  





Check 1: is every variation complete


Open the product. In Product data > Attributes, each attribute you sell by (Size, Colour) must have Used for variations ticked, or it never becomes a dropdown. Then open Variations and expand each one. A variation shows on the page only if it is Enabled, has a Regular price, and is in stock (or allows backorders). A variation with no price is silently skipped, and if every one is skipped the product shows no dropdowns at all. Also check each variation's attributes are set to real values; a combination with no variation behind it cannot be bought.



Check 2: is the page script running


Open the product logged out, in a private window, and pick each option. If the dropdowns appear but choosing one never shows a price or enables the button, the variation script did not run: open the browser console (F12) and look for a red error. A JavaScript error from another plugin, an optimisation plugin that delays or combines scripts, or a theme or page builder product template that leaves out the variation form all stop it. Products with more than 30 variations load their data on demand, so a security plugin, firewall or cache blocking ?wc-ajax= requests breaks them too.

Check it worked


In a private window, every size or colour you sell appears in the dropdowns. Picking a full combination shows that variation's price and stock and enables Add to cart, and the cart line names the chosen options. Try one combination you do not sell: it should be unavailable, not silently add the wrong item.



What not to do


    
  
  • Do not split each size into its own simple product to get round it. Stock, reviews and search all fragment.
    • 
  
  • Do not delete and regenerate all variations on a live product with orders. Past orders point at the old variations; fix the broken ones instead.
    • 
  
  • Do not turn off the optimisation plugin for the whole site. Exclude WooCommerce's scripts from it.

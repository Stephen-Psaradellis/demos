WooCommerce order emails not sending

Orders come in but you never get the New order email, or customers say they got no order confirmation, no receipt and no "your order has shipped" email. Some orders send and some do not, or every email lands in spam. A shop owner who misses a New order email ships late; a customer who gets no receipt opens a chargeback. Two checks tell you whether WooCommerce never sent the email or sent it and the mail server lost it.

Order #1042 notes

What the checks found


  





Check 1: is the email switched on, and was it triggered


Go to WooCommerce > Settings > Emails. Each email in the list (New order, Processing order, Completed order, Customer invoice...) has its own Enable this email notification box and, for the admin emails, its own Recipient(s) field. Open New order and check both: the box ticked and the recipient an address you actually read. Then open one order that sent nothing in WooCommerce > Orders and look at its status and the order notes on the right. The New order email fires only when an order moves from Pending payment or Failed to Processing or On hold. An order still in Pending payment or Failed never sent one, so the problem is the payment, not the email.



Check 2: did WordPress hand it to a mail server that delivered it


From the order page, use Order actions > Resend new order notification (or Send order details to customer) and click the arrow. Then send a test from any mail plugin you have, or install a free email log plugin first and resend: the log shows whether WordPress handed the email over and whether the handover failed. If the log says sent and nothing arrived, the email left your site and was dropped or filed as spam on the way, which is a sender-address and DNS problem. If the log shows an error, or WordPress has no working mailer at all, nothing ever left.

Check it worked


Place one real test order with a payment method you can refund (or a Cash on delivery method switched on for the test). Within a minute the owner's inbox should have the New order email and the customer's inbox the Processing order email, both in the inbox, not spam. Open the customer one and look at the sender: it should be your own domain. Then refund the test order and switch the test method off.



What not to do


    
  
  • Do not set the From address to your personal Gmail or Yahoo address. Those providers publish a policy telling every inbox to reject mail that claims to come from them but did not, so the emails disappear.
    • 
  
  • Do not install two SMTP plugins at once. Only one wins, and you will debug the one that is not sending.
    • 
  
  • Do not send customers a manual email for every order as a workaround. You will miss one on a busy day, and the missing receipts are exactly what turns into chargebacks.

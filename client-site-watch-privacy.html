Client Site Watch

Privacy policy and support for the Jira app. Last updated 2026-10-28.

Client Site Watch checks the public websites you list (your clients' sites) once a day and files what it finds as Jira issues in your project. This page says exactly what it keeps and what it sends, and how to reach the person who runs it.

What the app stores

  • The watch list: each site's address and the client name you type next to it. It is kept in Atlassian's Forge app storage for your site, not on any server of ours.
  • Jira issues it creates in the project you choose: one per problem found, with the evidence and fix steps. They are ordinary issues in your Jira and stay there under your control, including after you uninstall the app.
  • One Teamwork Graph document per watched site (the latest check result), so Rovo search can find it. It stays inside your Atlassian workspace.

The app keeps no user accounts, emails, names of Jira users, or Jira content outside your Atlassian site. There is no analytics or tracking inside the app.

Removing data

Remove a site from the watch list and it is deleted from storage and no longer checked. Uninstalling the app ends all checks; its Forge storage is then handled under Atlassian's platform rules. Issues it filed stay in your project until you delete them.
